PDF-Tool
Source: https://github.com/BK-2025/PDF-Tool

The macOS application includes CPython, PyMuPDF/MuPDF, Pillow, PyInstaller,
pyinstaller-hooks-contrib, altgraph, macholib and packaging. Their license
texts are included in the app bundle's licenses directory. PyInstaller's
bootloader exception applies to its generated application. PyMuPDF/MuPDF
is offered under AGPL or a commercial license; redistribution must satisfy
the applicable terms. This project does not grant a commercial license.

German and English OCR models are supplied from tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast:
https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast
